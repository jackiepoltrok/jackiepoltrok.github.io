#### Camera Details
Sony Cyber-shot DSC-P32 / 3.2MP / Digital Camera. Made and released in 2003, making it 23 years old.

---

The camera first belonged to my mother. I found it five years ago while cleaning out bins in our basement. It shockingly turned on, and I was able to find photos of my sister and I that it had saved from 2007. It was the camera that my mother used to document me and my sister's childhood.
I chose to use this camera to create a digital model for a variety of reasons. First, it is a very intricate object, with many small buttons and divots and details that could be challenging for a 3D model app to pick up. Second, a camera is in itself a digital archaology tool. Cameras are used to collect data in the format of photos that are used to digitally keep record of memories and artifacts, and of course, to create digital 3D models. And lastly, this camera is historically significant to me. It was passed down to me from my mother who used it in the early 2000s to document me and my sister’s upbringing. As an object, it has its own history and is an archaeological item. But as a tool, it has been used to keep track of my history for years.

# Comparison Criteria

| Criteria | What I am examining |
|---|---|
| Shape and structre | Does the model accurately reproduce the camera's overall form? |
| Surface detail | Are buttons, lenses, edges and other features visible? |
| Texture | Does the model capture the camera's surface and material appearance? |
| Missing info | Are there gaps, holes or areas that were not reconstructed? |
| Distortion | Are any parts stretched, warped or incorrectly shaped? |
| File quality | What file formats and levels of detail does each app produce? |
